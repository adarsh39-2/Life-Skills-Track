# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## 1. What types of behaviour can lead to sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment means any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes another person feel uncomfortable, offended, or unsafe. It can happen between colleagues, seniors, or even people outside the company.

There are different forms of sexual harassment:

### 1. Verbal Harassment
- Making inappropriate comments about someone's body or appearance.
- Making sexual jokes or using offensive language.
- Asking personal questions about someone's private life.
- Repeatedly asking someone for a date even after they say no.

### 2. Physical Harassment
- Touching, hugging, or patting someone without their permission.
- Standing too close to someone intentionally.
- Blocking someone's way or making unwanted physical contact.
- Any other unwanted physical behaviour.

### 3. Visual or Digital Harassment
- Sending inappropriate photos, videos, or messages.
- Sharing sexual content through emails or social media.
- Displaying offensive pictures, posters, or content at the workplace.
- Making unwanted sexual gestures or staring at someone in an uncomfortable way.

### 4. Quid Pro Quo Harassment
This happens when someone in a position of authority offers promotions, salary increases, or other work benefits in exchange for sexual favours. It can also include threatening someone's job if they refuse.

Sexual harassment can happen even through a single serious incident. It is not necessary for the behaviour to be repeated. The important thing is that the behaviour is unwelcome and affects the person.

## 2. What would I do if I face or witness such behaviour?

### If I experience such behaviour

1. I would clearly tell the person that their behaviour is inappropriate and ask them to stop.
2. I would keep a record of the incident, including the date, time and other details.
3. If it continues, I would report it in writing to MountBlue's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).

### If I witness such behaviour

- I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
- I would not ignore the incident and would share what I witnessed with the ICC.

### What happens after reporting?

- MountBlue follows a zero-tolerance policy towards sexual harassment.
- Action may include a warning, counselling, salary deduction or termination.
- Complaints are handled confidentially, and retaliation against the person reporting is not allowed.

I would respect everyone's personal space, avoid inappropriate jokes or comments, and maintain professional behaviour with everyone.

**Everyone deserves a safe and respectful workplace, and we should never ignore harassment.**
