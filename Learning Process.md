# Learning Process

### Question 1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning where we try to understand a topic by explaining it in simple words as if we are teaching someone else.

### Question 2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting thing I learned was that taking breaks while studying is also important. Sometimes when we are stuck on a problem, taking a break and doing something else helps us think better. Our brain can still work on a problem even when we are not actively thinking about it.

### Question 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active Mode:** When we focus completely on one task, like studying a topic or solving a coding problem.
- **Diffused Mode:** When we relax or take a break and our mind is free to think. During this time, our brain can connect different ideas and help us understand things better.

### Question 4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Decide what you want to learn.
2. Break the topic into smaller parts.
3. Learn the basics and start practising.
4. Remove distractions.
5. Practise consistently for at least 20 hours.
