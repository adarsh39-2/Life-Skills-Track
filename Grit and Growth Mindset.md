# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines.

- The video explains that grit is the combination of passion and hard work towards a long-term goal. It also tells us that talent alone is not enough to become successful; we need patience and should keep trying even when things get difficult.

## 2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

- The video explains the difference between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset. People with a fixed mindset think their abilities cannot change, whereas people with a growth mindset believe they can learn new things and improve themselves through practice and effort.

## 3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

- Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions are responsible for the results we get in life. The main point of the video is that we should not always blame luck or other people for our failures. Instead, we should focus on what we can control and keep working towards our goals.

## 4. What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?

- Believe that you can figure things out.
- Question your assumptions.
- Make your own learning plan.
- Accept difficulties and learn from them.

## 5. What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?

- **Take responsibility:** I will take responsibility for my learning and try to complete every task given to me.
- **Keep learning:** I will try to learn new things instead of thinking that I already know enough.
- **Try difficult tasks:** I will not avoid challenging problems because they help me learn more.
- **Learn from mistakes:** When I make a mistake, I will try to understand it and not repeat it again.
- **Ask for help:** If I get stuck somewhere, I will first try to find the solution myself and then ask others if needed.
- **Accept feedback:** I will listen to suggestions from others and work on improving my weak points.
- **Stay consistent:** I will practise regularly and keep working towards my goals, even if I don't get results immediately.

I know that improvement takes time, so I will keep learning, practising and trying to become better step by step.
